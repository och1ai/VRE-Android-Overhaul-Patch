# Changelog

## 0.1.3

* Mechanized androids are left out of the colonist tabs and are managed from the Mechs tab.
* The Mechs tab shows android energy as a value out of 100, the android name without the kind, and a working auto repair checkbox.
* Mechanized androids get an auto repair gizmo tied to the same setting.

## 0.1.2

* The Mechs tab no longer renders empty when an android is listed, and shows the android power level in the Energy column.

## 0.1.1

* Loading a saved androidtype keeps a single power source and blood type instead of selecting both the battery and the reactor.

## 0.1.0

First public release of the patch.

* Colonists repair wounds, scars and missing parts, and parts are no longer crafted.
* Androids can be destroyed, resurrected for 60 steel and 12 neutroamine, or reprinted from a subcore.
* Reactor or battery power, blood types with their own organs, optional memory hardware.
* The android assembler with a full body designer.
* New hardware and subroutines such as hemogenic, bloodless, coagulation, battery powered, spacer, heavy duty, delicate frame, hunter seeker, dull combat, time until shutdown, long jump, sleep cycle, gravpilot, ideological, occultist and mechanized.
* Mechanized androids with mechanitor oversight, work modes, escort and support for Mechanoid Engage Mode and WVC Work Modes.
* Fixes for the android stand in the rain and its posture, subroutine frames, synthetic skin faces and awakening passion.
